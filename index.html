About Us

Adebayo Yusuf Kehinde & Co. is a Nigerian legal practice committed to providing professional legal services to individuals and organisations.

Led by Adebayo Yusuf Kehinde, a Barrister and Solicitor of the Supreme Court of Nigeria, the practice draws on 20 years of legal experience and practical knowledge.

Mr. Kehinde attended Holy Cross Primary School, Lagos Island, and Okesuna High School, Lafiaji. He subsequently studied at Usmanu Danfodiyo University, Sokoto, and the Nigerian Law School. He was called to the Nigerian Bar in 2007.

He began his legal practice in 2007 with MBA Ismail & Co. at No. 28, Joseph Street, off Tokunboh Street, Lagos Island. Following the relocation of MBA Ismail & Co. to the United Kingdom, he retained the office and continued practising under the name Adebayo Yusuf Kehinde & Co.

The practice provides services in litigation, property matters, preparation of legal documents, and legal advisory services across various fields of law.
